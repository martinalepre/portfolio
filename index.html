About

Raised under Naples' sun, quick on my feet ever since. Now building that pace into Dutch structure.

My background is in psychology: a Master's in Psychology of Communication and Marketing from Sapienza University of Rome, on top of a Bachelor's in Psychological Sciences. That's what I use before writing a single caption. Understand the audience first, then write for them.

I went freelance in February 2025 and worked with healthcare and retail clients until December 2025, running the whole process myself: onboarding, strategy, content calendars, publishing, reporting. Before that, an in-house role at Goeldlin and front-office work at a beauty center taught me the other side. Working inside a team, on deadlines that weren't mine to set.

AI is a big part of how I work. Not as a shortcut, but as a way to close gaps. When there's something I don't know how to do yet, a website, a specific design, a new tool, I use AI to learn it fast and get it done properly. This site is proof: I'd never written a line of HTML before, and I built it with AI as my working partner.

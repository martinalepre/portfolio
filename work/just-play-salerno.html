Just Play Salerno · Streetwear Retail, Freelance

Reviving a dormant account for a brand-new store

The Salerno store had opened less than a year earlier. The Instagram account had gone quiet right when it needed people to notice it.

Client: Just Play Salerno, Salerno
Role: Freelance Social Media Strategist
Timeline: 2025
Channels: Instagram, TikTok

Overview

Just Play Salerno is a streetwear retail brand. Their Salerno location had opened less than a year before I came on, and my brief was to handle their content and communication across Instagram and TikTok.

The Challenge

The Instagram account had gone inactive right when a brand-new store needed an audience around it. No posting rhythm, no warmed-up following, and a Salerno location still building its name. Streetwear audiences also expect a specific visual language, and generic retail content wouldn't hold their attention.

My Role

Content strategy and creation for both channels: rebuilding a posting rhythm from close to zero and shaping it around the brand's streetwear identity.

Process

  • Rebuilt the content calendar from scratch, prioritising the posts that could build visibility fastest for a store still new to most of its potential audience.
  • Developed content aligned with the brand's streetwear identity and its target audience, across Instagram and TikTok.
  • Managed the account as one of two concurrent freelance clients, alongside CNO's healthcare content.

Where it stands

The top Reel reached 4,705 organic views in month one, zero ad spend, a real sign of life for an account that had gone quiet. But the honest read is that a dormant account doesn't fully recover in a single sprint: the account is active again, in step with a store still establishing itself in Salerno, and that's the case for continuing to invest in it as the store builds its local presence.
